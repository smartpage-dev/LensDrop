1. What changed

The Android Event screen now opens a native, three-column, scrollable photo gallery. The Guest Delivery screen has an always-visible, two-row action dock. The watch-folder upload queue, Hostinger archive, Group/Single QR identities and Windows/58B3 printing protocols are preserved.

Why no QR appeared automatically: a new watched photo is privately archived but not assigned to a guest. A guest QR requires a prepared cloud delivery link and an explicit Group or Single selection. H5.8 offers a one-tap Next Group path that prepares missing links online and starts a group; it never guesses a guest from folder order.

2. Event → Open photo gallery

The gallery displays up to 30 photos initially in three columns, with More photos up to 90 on the current page. While the screen is visible, the app checks the local queue every 5 seconds and rebuilds the grid only when the photo set, upload state, assignment or star status changes. Thumbnails load on at most two worker threads and use a 12 MiB memory cache.

3. Guest QR — fixed daily controls

On Guest Delivery, the QR preview and receiving/shown group summary sit above the scrollable advanced settings. The six common controls stay visible in a fixed two-row dock:

Prev QRCurrent QRNext GroupSelect PhotoPrint PhotoPrint QR

Prev QR shows an older guest QR without changing the receiving group. Current QR restores the QR for the receiving group. Next Group creates the next receiving group; if no prepared links exist, it prepares them online first. Select Photo opens the candidate picker, and in Single mode prepares links online when necessary. Print actions open their existing confirmation screens. Long-history selection, quick Undo, mode switching and trusted-order configuration remain available in the scrollable settings, not the fixed dock.

Important: selecting a photo for a guest is separate from the cloud upload and from public gallery publication. Printing a ticket is not a photo-print order. The same guest QR is intended for online access and staff-operated photo lookup/collection.

5. Controlled phone test

  1. Install the new APK using the same signer; verify code 14 in the app.
  2. With a disposable event and new-only watch folder, upload three JPEGs. Open Gallery and verify three columns, scrolling, automatic new-photo appearance and per-photo upload states.
  3. Open an image, enlarge, star, show info, hide, toggle Show hidden, and restore. Verify the upload queue and cloud photo are unchanged.
  4. Open Guest Delivery with no group. Tap Next Group once with internet available. Confirm a QR appears and the receiving group is named. Assign one photo and verify the QR opens the intended personal page after release.
  5. Tap Prev QR, Current QR, and Next Group. Ensure showing an old QR does not move incoming photos to the old group.
  6. From a gallery photo, open Print Photo; verify the existing preview/profile/copies screen and simulate one print. Do not use physical paper initially.
  7. From an assigned gallery photo, open Print QR; verify the correct shown guest and confirm a single ticket manually. Scan the printed full URL.
  8. Pause/reopen the app, check starred/hidden flags, original assignments and queue IDs. Test 4G/5G and offline QR history separately.

Unverified: native Android compilation/lint on this revision, real phone layouts and image decoding, 58B3 ticket scanning, CP9550 output and long-duration multi-photographer resource behavior. Local JVM and source checks are not a replacement for these tests.
